Disaster Safety Guidelines

🌋 Earthquakes

DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON. Stay indoors under a heavy table until the shaking stops. Move away from glass panels and outer walls.

🌊 Floods

Move immediately to higher ground. Avoid driving or walking through flooded pathways. Keep a close watch on official flash flood warnings.

🔥 Fires

Stay low under smoke and crawl to safety. Test doors for heat before turning handles. Establish a family meeting spot safely outdoors.

Emergency Survival Kit

Ensure your Family Pack is Equipped.

  • day water supply
  • Non Perishable Food
  • First aid medical kit with personal medication
  • Flashlight with replacement batteries
  • Battery operated or hand crank portable radio
